LUXIAL respects the privacy of everyone who visits this site and of every owner who trusts us with their property. This policy sets out plainly what data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what you can require of us at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is LUXIAL, Lda., company number 000 000 000, registered at Lagos, Algarve, Portugal.

For anything concerning your personal data, including exercising the rights in section 8, write to luxial.gestao.al@gmail.com or to the postal address above.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as none of the circumstances in Article 37 GDPR applies to us.

2. What we collect

Data you give us

When you complete a form on this site, whether the request for an assessment or the contact form, we collect:

  • your name, email and phone number, which are required because without them we cannot reply to you;
  • the property address and your message, both optional;
  • any details about the property you choose to give us, such as the town, the property type and the number of bedrooms;
  • a record of your consent, including the date and time you gave it.

Data collected automatically

The server hosting this site keeps the usual web server log: the visitor’s IP address, the date and time, the address requested, the response code and the browser identification. These records exist to keep the site running, diagnose faults and stop abuse. We do not use them to identify you and we do not combine them with form submissions.

What we do not collect

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, we do not use ad networks, we do not buy lists, and we do not collect special categories of data (health, beliefs, ethnic origin or any other). Nor do we store anything in your browser beyond what the page you are viewing strictly needs.

3. Why we use your data, on what basis, and for how long

Replying to a contact or income estimate request

  • Data: identification, contact details and property details.
  • Basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and the pre-contractual steps you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Retention: 24 months after our last contact with you.

Providing the property management service

  • Data: identification, contact, property and contract details.
  • Basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Retention: for the duration of the contract.

Meeting legal obligations, meaning invoicing, tax and accounting duties, reporting guests to the authorities and tourist tax

  • Data: whatever each rule requires.
  • Basis: a legal obligation to which we are subject (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Retention: the applicable statutory periods, generally 10 years for tax and accounting records.

Keeping the site running and protecting it from abuse

  • Data: server logs (IP address, date and time, page requested).
  • Basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: 6 months.

Understanding, in aggregate, which pages are useful

  • Data: aggregated metrics, no identification.
  • Basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: indefinitely, as it no longer identifies you.

Where the basis is consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without giving a reason, and without affecting the lawfulness of what was done beforehand.

4. Cookies and usage statistics

This site sets no cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or any other service that creates a persistent visitor identifier. That is why you will not find a cookie banner here: there would be nothing to ask you for.

If traffic analysis is switched on later, it will be a service we host ourselves (Plausible or Umami), which sets no cookies, does not record the full IP address, creates no identifier across visits and cannot identify a person. If we ever adopt something that does require consent, we will ask for it first and update this policy.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent or pass on personal data for third-party marketing. We share only what is necessary, and only with:

  • our server hosting provider, which hosts the site and the service that receives the forms;
  • our email provider, through which your request reaches us;
  • our accountants, where the data appears on documents with tax relevance;
  • booking platforms (Airbnb, Booking.com) and local cleaning and maintenance providers, exclusively where you are a client and only as far as managing your property requires;
  • public authorities, where the law requires it, in particular guest reporting to AIMA and obligations towards the Portuguese tax authority.

Every provider processing data on our behalf is bound by a processing agreement under Article 28 GDPR.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

We aim to have your data processed within the European Economic Area. Where any of the providers above processes data outside the EEA, that transfer is made under a European Commission adequacy decision or under standard contractual clauses, and you may ask us for a copy of the safeguards in place.

7. Security

The site is served exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Forms are validated on the server and protected against automated submissions. Access to the requests we receive is limited to the people at LUXIAL who need them in order to reply. No system is infallible, but we treat your data with the care we would expect for our own.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights at all times:

  • access: to know what data we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • rectification: to correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erasure: to have data deleted when it is no longer needed, or when you withdraw consent;
  • restriction: to have processing paused while a dispute is resolved;
  • portability: to receive the data you gave us in an open format, or to have us send it to another organisation;
  • objection: to object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdrawal of consent: at any time, without giving a reason.

To exercise any of them, write to luxial.gestao.al@gmail.com. We reply within one month, extendable by a further two months only for particularly complex requests and always with notice. We may need to confirm your identity first, precisely in order to protect your data.

If you believe we have not handled the matter as we should, you have the right to complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134 – 1.º, 1200-651 Lisbon, www.cnpd.pt.

9. Children

This site is aimed at property owners and adult travellers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you know that a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we publish the updated version here and change the last updated date shown at the top. Where a change is substantial and affects data you have already entrusted to us, we notify anyone with an ongoing relationship with us by email.
