Estimate income

02First steps

From the first contact to the first guest.

We do not ask for exclusivity before showing you numbers.

  1. 01

    Conversation and visit

    We see the property, understand what you want from it, and tell you frankly what we think it earns. If it does not make sense for you, we tell you right there on the visit.

  2. 02

    Licensing and preparation

    We check the Alojamento Local licence or handle the registration, the mandatory insurance and the complaints book. We point out only the adjustments the house needs before it opens its doors.

  3. 03

    Photography and listing

    A professional photo shoot and a listing designed to show the property at its best and generate bookings.

  4. 04

    Pricing and channels

    We adjust rates daily to demand to maximise your earnings. We publish on Airbnb, Booking.com and for direct bookings, with calendars always synchronised.

  5. 05

    Day-to-day operation

    We take on the whole routine: guest support, check-ins, cleaning, on-site maintenance and legal obligations.

  6. 06

    Monthly statement

    We send a transparent statement at the end of the month with revenue, costs and occupancy. No surprises, and nothing you have to ask for.

Want to know what your property could earn?

Tell us where the property is and what it has. We send a detailed estimate, with no obligation and no exclusivity until there is a contract.
