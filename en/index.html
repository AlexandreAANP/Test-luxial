01About us

There are two of us. We are from the Algarve. And we created LUXIAL.

We started with a simple idea: take everything a holiday rental involves and carry that responsibility for the owners.

From strategy and pricing to bookings, guests, cleaning, maintenance and all the unexpected things that are part of everyday life.

We work between Portimão and Alvor, and we know the market we are in.

But more important than saying who we are is showing what we do.

Well-kept homes. Happy guests. Owners who are looked after. And management designed to get the best out of every property.

02What we do for you

Seven responsibilities that stop being yours.

We take on operational and strategic management from start to finish. Your only role is to follow the results at the end of each month.

  1. 01

    Listing and photography

    Professional photography and video to make the property stand out and attract more bookings on the platforms.

  2. 02

    Dynamic pricing and channels

    Rates adjusted daily to demand and season on Airbnb, Booking.com and for direct bookings.

  3. 03

    Guest support

    Fast answers to questions and requests in Portuguese, English and Spanish, before, during and after the stay.

  4. 04

    Check-in and check-out

    Meet-and-greet or self check-in, with document verification and an inspection of the property at departure.

  5. 05

    Cleaning and laundry

    Thorough cleaning and professionally laundered linen at every changeover, so the property is always immaculate.

  6. 06

    Maintenance

    Frequent inspections and fast problem-solving. Major repairs only go ahead with your approval.

  7. 07

    Legal compliance

    Alojamento Local registration, guest reporting to the authorities, complaints book, municipal tourist tax, mandatory insurance and invoicing. We take these obligations off your desk.

03An honest comparison

Self-management or professional management.

Self-managing costs no commission. If you live nearby, have the time and enjoy the work, it can pay. The difference shows up in the empty weeks, the August breakdown, and the rules that keep changing.

On your ownWith LUXIAL
Your time
On your ownHours every week in high season, plus out-of-hours emergencies.
With LUXIALApproving certain work and reading the statement.
Nightly rate
On your ownA fixed rate per season, adjusted now and then when there is time to spare.
With LUXIALConstant rate optimisation based on the market, occupancy and local events.
Guest response
On your ownDepends on your daily availability. A slow reply is a lost booking.
With LUXIALFast service from people in the area, with fluent support in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
Cost
On your ownNo commission. But empty nights, last-minute cleans and repairs at emergency rates.
With LUXIALA commission on what the property earns. If it earns nothing, we charge nothing.
Legal compliance
On your ownYours to carry: AL registration, AIMA, tourist tax, insurance, invoicing. The fines too.
With LUXIALWe handle the registration, the filings and the deadlines, and keep proof of all of it.
