About the house

A one-bedroom apartment in Três Castelos, at the western end of Praia da Rocha, the quieter stretch of the seafront. It is away from the bustle of the main avenue, but only minutes from it.

A bright, simple interior with plants and a few framed travel posters. From the balcony you see palms, rooftops and the sea beyond. The beach is a few minutes' walk away.

The building has a pool and a tennis court. A garage space is included at no cost, which is rare in Praia da Rocha in August. Check-in is self-service and digital, at whatever hour suits.

Sleeps up to four: one bedroom with a double bed, and a sofa bed in the living room. There is a dedicated workspace, for anyone staying longer than a week.
